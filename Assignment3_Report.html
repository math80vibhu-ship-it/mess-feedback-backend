2(d) Threat Agents & GDPR Regulatory Compliance

  • Applicable Regulation: General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) / Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). The system collects and processes personal data (names, registration roll numbers, phone numbers, and dietary/hygiene feedback) of natural persons.
  • Threat Agent 1 (Malicious Insider / Rogue Mess Worker):
    • Attack: Tampering with dining schedules or deleting student complaints to avoid penalties.
    • Control: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) + TOTP Multi-Factor Authentication.
    • GDPR Article 32(1)(b) (Security of Processing): Enforces authorization barriers ensuring confidentiality and data integrity.
  • Threat Agent 2 (External Cybercriminal / Credential Stuffing & Interception):
    • Attack: Automated credential stuffing using stolen credential dumps, or intercepting database storage.
    • Control: Salted Bcrypt Password Hashing (10 rounds) + 30s Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP) + Non-root Alpine execution.
    • GDPR Article 32(1)(a) & Article 25 (Data Protection by Design): Mandates cryptographic hashing/encryption and technical safeguards against unauthorized data extraction.
